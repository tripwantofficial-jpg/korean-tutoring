## Your first week 🌱

Welcome! For the next 4 weeks, we're going to turn "slow letter-by-letter reading" into **automatic Korean reading and writing**. This first week is all about making your brain recognize each 자모 (jamo) instantly.

**The rule for this week:**
- **Every day, 15 minutes.** Same time if possible.
- **On a busy day: even 5 minutes** is better than skipping.
- **Finished early and want more?** Send me your work + 🙋 on KakaoTalk. I'll send extra.

---

## Your practice schedule (6 days)

### 📅 Monday — 15 min · Dictation (받아쓰기)

**What you'll get from me**: A voice message with 20 Korean syllables (no batchim).

**What to do**:
1. Listen to the audio.
2. Write each syllable in your notebook. Just write what you hear — don't worry about meaning.
3. Take a photo and send it back on KakaoTalk.

**Tip**: If you miss one, don't rewind. Write "?" and move on. It's data for me.

---

### 📅 Tuesday — 15 min · Speed Reading (속도 읽기)

**What you'll get from me**: 60-syllable sheet (Sheet A).

**What to do**:
1. Read the whole sheet aloud, top to bottom. **Time yourself.** Write down the seconds.
2. Do it again. Time yourself.
3. Do it a third time. Time yourself.
4. Send me your 3 times (e.g., "180 → 145 → 120").

**Goal**: The third time should be faster than the first. That's the whole point.

---

### 📅 Wednesday — 20 min · Jamo Combining (자모 조합 쓰기)

**What you'll get from me**: A list of 20 combinations like this:
```
1. ㄱ + ㅘ =
2. ㅁ + ㅕ =
3. ㅃ + ㅜ =
...
```

**What to do**:
1. Combine each into a proper syllable block in your notebook.
2. Remember the rule:
   - Vertical vowel (ㅏ ㅓ ㅣ 등) → consonant on **left**
   - Horizontal vowel (ㅗ ㅜ ㅡ 등) → consonant on **top**
   - Complex vowel (ㅘ ㅝ ㅢ 등) → consonant on **top-left**
3. Take a photo and send it.

---

### 📅 Thursday — 15 min · Dictation Round 2 (복모음 집중)

**What you'll get from me**: A voice message with 20 syllables, but this time **complex vowels** are mixed in (왜, 예, 뛔, 놔 etc.).

**What to do**:
1. Same as Monday. Listen, write, send photo.
2. Complex vowels are the hardest part — don't stress if you get them wrong. That's why we're practicing.

---

### 📅 Friday — 15 min · Speed Reading Round 2 (속도 읽기)

**What you'll get from me**: 60-syllable sheet (Sheet B) — different from Tuesday.

**What to do**:
1. Same as Tuesday. Read aloud, time yourself, 3 rounds.
2. Send me your times.

**Goal**: Try to beat your Tuesday time. If not, that's still fine — we're building up.

---

### 📅 Saturday — 15 min · Mini Test (미니 테스트) 🎯

**What you'll get from me**: 
- A voice message with 10 syllables (dictation)
- An image with 10 syllables to read aloud

**What to do**:
1. Write the 10 dictation syllables → photo
2. Record yourself reading the 10 image syllables → voice message
3. Send both to me

**What this is**: Not a test to judge you. A **mirror** to show us both what's still shaky. Next week we fix it.

---

### 📅 Sunday — Rest 🍵

You've earned it. Or reply 🙋 to my Sunday message and I'll send bonus practice.

## What NOT to do this week

- ❌ Don't try to memorize word meanings. **Sound first, meaning later.**
- ❌ Don't use Papago to check your writing. Your mistakes are our data.
- ❌ Don't try to catch up on 3 missed days at once. Just start today.
- ❌ Don't feel bad if you can't read fast yet. Nobody can. You're building a new brain circuit.

## What TO do

- ✅ Do the daily 15 minutes. Even if imperfect.
- ✅ Submit even partial work. **On time > polished.**
- ✅ Message me anytime with questions.
- ✅ Read Korean anywhere you see it — signs, Netflix subtitles, K-pop lyrics. Just try to sound it out. That's practice too.

Week 2: Read words with batchim.
Week 3: Read/write with 연음 (sound changes).
Week 4: Handle real content — song lyrics, subtitles, menus.
